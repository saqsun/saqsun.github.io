About

I’m a hands-on engineer and technical leader with 15+ years across HTML5 games, frontend platforms, and developer tooling. My experience includes Playrix, RubyPlay, Voodoo, and Ixilix, alongside co-founding Armath. I’ve shipped browser games and build open-source tools like reparticle and Replayable. I care about reusable architecture, browser performance, and helping engineering teams do their best work.
